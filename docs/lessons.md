# Lessons (evidence behind the pipeline Gotchas)

No stage reads this file. Each pipeline doc keeps the one-line rule in its Gotchas; the story behind it (project, measurements, what happened) lives here, verbatim. Script paths in older stories are the per-project copies of that time; the tracked versions are in `tools/`.

## take-selection

- A retake cut that starts at Whisper's word end can swallow the next word (`sdai-12`: "wants Hindi, for" lost "Hindi"; Whisper put "Hindi" 0.3 s early). Before trusting the cut voice, diff its words against the script and decode each cut edge with a CTC window plus 20 ms energy.
- MFA word edges can sit inside speech (about 1 in 5 cuts on `sql-vs-nosql`). Snap every cut to measured silence: the first quiet 10 ms run (≥ 60 ms, below room tone + 10 dB) after the last kept word, and the last one before the next. Then check that the 20 ms at every edge is near room tone.
- Don't start the silence search before the word's end, or a stop-consonant closure inside the word (the "d" in "decide") reads as silence.
- A false start often runs straight into its retake with no silence ("…interviewer then the interviewer asked"). Search for silence on each side of the removed span separately; searching the whole gap finds the pause BEFORE the false start and keeps it in. Where there is no silence, cut on the MFA edge, then nudge to the 10 ms energy dip within ±100 ms and list those cuts for a listen (`bloom-filter`).
- MFA parks the first word in the leading silence; take Whisper's time for it. Don't "fix" other words from Whisper, because Whisper collapses repeats ("you say, you say").
- Render cuts with `trim=start_frame/end_frame` and `atrim=start_sample/end_sample` (1,600 samples per frame at 48 kHz). Time-based `trim` sometimes takes one boundary frame too many, and `concat` then pads the audio to match (+10 frames over 48 cuts).
- Teleprompter recordings: pauses carry room noise and breaths around −45 dB, so the "room tone + 10 dB" silence threshold finds no silence and keeps multi-second gaps. Raise the margin until the pauses go (20 dB on `kafka` and `redis-invalidation`, speech ≈ −12 dB), then check every cut edge's 20 ms is below −35 dB and nudge the rest to the quietest frame boundary within ±100 ms.
- MFA also parks short words ("then", "the", "one") on breaths inside long pauses, which keeps the pause as "speech". Look for words that start > 1 s from Whisper's time and sit at < −30 dB; pin them from the 50 ms energy envelope (`time_fix` in `config/build_timeline.json`) rather than trusting either aligner.
- Whisper can invent repeats ("cache cash logic"): settle every "repeat" with CTC before cutting it (`redis-invalidation`: one "cache").
- Don't align a whole recording as one MFA utterance. On `rate-limiter` (5:27, one utterance) MFA drifted 1–8 s around retakes and put a kept retake inside the cut take. Split the corpus into utterances at real pauses (energy gaps ≥ 0.5 s; place each word by its Whisper end − 0.1 s, since Whisper stretches starts back into silence; merge one-word chunks) and align a TextGrid. Then compare MFA to a CTC greedy decode over every chunk: median offset ≈ −0.07 s, and any word off by > 0.35 s marks a problem.
- All Whisper passes (plain, VAD, disfluency) can collapse a real restart ("once someone logs in, the… once someone logs in, limit…"; "user, key… user, key, or phone"). The CTC chunk scan (difflib against the corpus, look for inserted words) found both on `rate-limiter`; MFA drift in a stretch with no retake is the tell.
- One-take teleprompter reads (no retakes, `ag-ui`): skip MFA. An energy EDL is enough: 10 ms RMS, speech threshold −24 dB (±2 frame smoothing), compress gaps to 0.25 s, then snap each cut to the quietest 30 fps frame boundary inside its gap (plain rounding put 15 edges into speech). Don't merge short "breath" bursts between gaps: a −19 dB burst was the soft "th" of "they" and merging it clipped the word.
- Before cutting a "stumble" Whisper reports inside continuous speech, check the duration and energy: on `oidc`, "OpenID 2 bundle 2.0" was 0.9 s of unbroken speech (no dip below -17 dB), the natural length of "OpenID 2.0", and pass B heard just that. MFA squeezing the extra words into 0.03–0.07 s each is the tell. Don't cut it.
- Two-part recordings: one `build_edl` config per part (its own `start`/`end`, `cuts` and `out`); the tool exits on a cut outside the part's range. List `build_timeline` `time_fix` pins latest-first, or a moved word can make a later pin for the same word ambiguous.
- Re-transcribe the cut voice (plate `voice.wav`) and read it against `script.md` before composing. On `rag-pipeline`, all three source passes collapsed "evaluation, measure… measure retrieval" (3 s apart) into one "measure"; the energy EDL then cut "evaluation" and kept a stray "measure". Only the cut-voice pass showed it ("ask about measure measure retrieval").
- Rendering a cut's PICTURE with one `trim` branch per segment from a single 1080x1920 input buffers every branch's frames in RAM: 44 segments hit the kernel OOM killer at 16 GB (`jwt-explained`, exit 137). Audio `atrim` branches are fine. For picture use one `select='between(n,f0,f1-1)+…',setpts=N/(30*TB)` stream (frame-exact, no buffering).
- Look at frame 0 of the cut: on `jwt-is-dead` it landed mid-blink, 0.1 s before the first word. Start the first segment on an open-eyes frame a few frames earlier (`first_in` in the EDL config), then shift the word times by the added frames / speed.
- Breaths between sentences can sit at about −30 dB and pass a −32 dB speech threshold as a 0.6–0.8 s "speech" run, which leaves ~1.2 s of dead air after the cut (`system-design-of-ai/01-streaming`: after "same flow" and "as it thinks"). The cut-voice Whisper pass shows them as a word lasting > 1 s. Map that output time back through the EDL, check the source energy (a flat −30 dB plateau with no −13 dB peaks is a breath), and add an explicit cut for it.
- A beat pause the brief asks for ("half-beat before 'But'") may have no source silence to stretch: on `system-design-of-ai/03`, "search.But" was spoken with a 40 ms gap and the 1.1 s pause came after "But", and Whisper put "But" on the next word ("being"), so a `long_before` hold landed between "But" and "being". Find the word from the energy runs, not Whisper; then split at the dip and splice in a slice of real pause from the same take (`hold_inserts: [[split_t, sil_a, sil_b]]` in the `build_edl` config). Cover the two jump cuts with the HEAD punch-in.
- Phone recordings in the home room (`system-design-of-ai/08-long-running-requests`): breaths between beats sat at −29…−34 dB for 0.4–0.6 s, so a −32 dB speech threshold kept five 1.1–1.4 s gaps (the cut-voice Whisper showed "but", "so" lasting > 1 s). Raising `thresh_db` to −29 removed them (163.9 s → 158.2 s) with every edge still ≤ −35 dB after `nudge_edges.py`. Check the cut-voice Whisper for words > 0.9 s before accepting a cut.
- Snapping sentence starts to measured onsets (`build_cfg.py`): use the same speech threshold as the EDL (−29 dB on the phone setup), and search up to +0.9 s after Whisper's start. Whisper puts sentence starts on the breath before them (up to 0.4 s early: "Last" at 35.22 s, speech at 35.6 s), so a ±0.4 s window found no onset for 26 of 63 starts on `system-design-of-ai/10-batch-inference` and those gaps fell back to the 0.15 s within-sentence pause.
- CTC parks the first and last words early ("Namoshkar!" 0.07 s long, "sayonara" 0.36 s before its energy). Pin them from the 10 ms energy envelope (`pins` in `config/build_timeline.json`) before anchoring the opener hold or the end card on them (`system-design-of-ai/10-batch-inference`).
- `build_edl`: `end` stays at `last_out` (sayonara end + ~1.25 s; the default when `end` is omitted), never the source length. On `system-design-of-ai/11-max-tokens-and-truncation` a sound after the wave (246.97 s) became a speech run past `last_out` (245.3 s); `last_out` then set that segment's end before its start, and the voice came out 1.66 s longer than the EDL with the recording's tail appended. After every EDL build, assert no segment has `end <= start` and that `sum(frames)` equals the rendered voice length.
- Whisper can merge a retake of a short line into one sentence: "Your stopped mid-sentence… Your AI stopped mid-sentence" came back as one "Your AI stopped mid-sentence" spanning both takes (`11-max-tokens-and-truncation`, 16.7–19.1 s). Run the windowed CTC source decode (`ctc_windows.py`, 8 s / 7 s hop) before choosing takes; it heard both attempts.
- Whisper word times can drift seconds off on a long teleprompter read (`system-design-of-ai/09-prompt-caching`: "So remember" 245.1 vs real 250.9 s, "Namoshkar" 5.54 vs 3.44 s), which put a 0.5 s hold on a breath. Snap sentence starts from the windowed CTC source decode (`ctc_src.txt`, onset = last energy start within −0.45/+0.06 s of the CTC time) instead.
- Hook-first reorder of a first-recorded "Namoshkar!": `insert_after: [[hook_end, a, b]]` in the `build_edl.py` config moves the source range to right after the hook segment (`09-prompt-caching`).

## transcribe

- Whisper confidently "fixes" misspoken words to the script. When the aligner and the audio disagree with Whisper, trust the audio.
- Keep logs in `work/logs/`; the console output is huge.
- The VAD pass can drop a whole sentence (on `redis-interview` it dropped a 3 s interviewer question) and merge two takes across a part join. Re-transcribe those windows on their own; their word times are better than either full pass.
- whisper-ctranslate2 can die with "CUDA failed with error out of memory" late in a file while still printing "Transcription results written" and leaving the output folder empty. Check the JSON exists and grep the log for "out of memory". On `bloom-filter` the prompt-conditioned pass A died at 2:52 twice on an otherwise idle 4 GB card (also at `int8`); `--condition_on_previous_text False` fixed it.
- The VAD pass (B) can also invent sentences in pauses ("In the end, it was a total failure.", `rate-limiter`). Treat any B-only line as a hallucination until CTC confirms it.
- Whisper word STARTS on the final (cut, sped-up) voice run early: median +0.20 s (p90 +0.38 s) on `scion`, because it stretches each start back into the preceding pause. Captions and every word-triggered visual landed early. Fix before building the timeline: CTC greedy-decode the whole voice (wav2vec2, 20 s windows), match words with difflib, take the CTC onset − 0.03 s, interpolate the offset for unmatched words (`scion` `work/scripts/align_ctc.py`, 70% matched). Spot-check 3 words against a CTC window decode afterwards.
- wav2vec2 CTC (`wav2vec2-large-960h-lv60-self`) in fp16 returns ALL blanks for windows over ~10 s (no error, 0 words; `jwt-explained`). Run it in fp32 with 8 s windows + 1 s overlap (~1.5 GB, fits beside another job), and reject CTC−Whisper offsets outside −0.3…+0.8 s as difflib mismatches (interpolate those instead).
- The cut-voice Whisper pass can ALSO collapse a retake: on `docker-is-dead` it read "He says Docker is dead, and in 2022, Kubernetes… He says Docker is dead and in 2022 Kubernetes really did stop using it" as one sentence, so 3.5 s of a false start survived into the voice. Before building captions, run a windowed CTC decode of the whole cut voice (8 s windows, 7 s hop) and diff it against `script.md` (`docker-is-dead` `work/scripts/ctc_scan.py`): any REPLACE/INSERT block that is longer on the CTC side than the script and isn't a number/acronym spelling ("o c i", "two fifty six") is a hidden repeat. Fix the EDL, then re-run the voice chain.
- Whisper can hide a whole retake inside one stretched word: on `jwt-is-dead` "assertions." ran 2.4 s and swallowed a 3.3 s retake of the same line, leaving 2.5 s of apparent dead air after the cut. After building the timeline, list every word longer than 1 s and CTC-decode its window before trusting it.
- CTC onsets more than +0.45 s after Whisper's start are usually difflib mismatches (`jwt-is-dead`: "default." landed on Whisper's END of the word, so captions ran 0.6–0.8 s late near the end). Don't take Whisper + offset blindly either: sentence starts after a pause are where Whisper stretches back. Take the energy onset between the two times (first rise above −28 dB after ≥ 60 ms below −35 dB), else Whisper + the median offset (`jwt-is-dead` `work/scripts/clamp_ctc.py`).
- CTC onsets (`align_ctc.py`, onset − 0.03 s) still ran a median 0.115 s LATE (p90 0.31 s) against the real speech onset on `docker-is-dead` (1.30x voice), measured on the 103 words that follow a pause. video-inspect's `caption_desync` flags are dominated by its own Whisper running early, so don't trust those numbers; measure against the energy envelope instead. Fix: `work/scripts/retime_onsets.py` snaps each pause-led word to its quiet→loud onset (≥ 50 ms below −35 dB, then > −28 dB, searched in [s − 0.4, s + 0.15] s) minus 0.03 s and interpolates the correction for the words in between (median after: −0.03 s). Run it on `timeline.json` before the first render; every `at()` trigger moves with it.
- Sentence-first words stayed 0.5–1.26 s EARLY after `align_ctc.py` + `clamp_ctc.py` + `retime_onsets.py` on `vectorless-rag` (1.00x voice, one-take read): difflib dropped them from the CTC match, the interpolated offset was too small, and `retime_onsets` clamps at +0.40 s. "But there's a catch" would have cut to the dark punch 1.1 s before I said it. Fix (`vectorless-rag` `work/scripts/build_timeline.py`, "ctc refine" block): match the SCRIPT tokens against the literal windowed CTC decode (`ctc_windows.py` → `ctc_full.txt`, already made for the repeat scan), take the energy onset − 0.02 s only when the word leads after a pause (onset later than the previous CTC word), else CTC time − 0.06 s; drop corrections outside −0.35…+1.4 s as mismatches and interpolate the rest. Then list every word whose start differs from its CTC time by > 0.3 s: it should be a handful of number/acronym tokens, never a beat's first word. Again on Sentence-first words still landed 0.3–0.9 s EARLY on the previous word's tail after `align_ctc` + `clamp_ctc` + `retime_onsets` on `system-design-of-ai/04-cost-metering` (13 words, every beat switch among them: "But behind" at 43.17 s instead of 44.05 s). Fix: `work/scripts/fix_starts.py` on `timeline.json` after the retime (in `compose_data.sh`). It moves a word only when the previous script token ends in `.,:?!`, a ≥ 0.15 s quiet run (< −36 dB) begins within 0.45 s of its start, the NEXT word also starts after that run ("Read it." stays), and speech resumes within 1.2 s; new start = onset − 0.03 s. Test punctuation on the raw script token and skip hidden display tokens, or a merged "25¢" (hidden "cents.") jumps onto the next sentence.
- `retime_onsets.py` looked back 0.4 s for a quiet→loud onset from every word. On a 1.30x voice (`system-design-of-ai/02-token-rate-limiting`) that reached past the previous word, so mid-sentence words snapped to the sentence's first onset: 98 caption words collapsed onto their neighbour's frame ("A hundred requests" all at 0.02 s). Never search before the previous word's start + 0.08 s (fixed in that project's `work/scripts/retime_onsets.py`: 13 near-collisions left, all short function words). After the retime, count words that start < 2 frames after the previous one.
- Word times in `timeline.json` are fractional frames after the retime. Round inside `at()` and round any frame passed to `renderStill` ("Argument for frame must be an integer").
- Whisper silently drops short code-mixed Hindi phrases on the cut voice ("Now kab khulega and when do you open" came back as "now and when do you open" on `sdai-03-semantic-caching`) and stretches the neighbouring word over the gap ("now" spanned 0.9 s). The script-token timeline then squeezes the missing words into ~0.1 s. Check every `MISSING`/`FILLED` line from `build_timeline.py`, decode that window with `ctc_window.py` ("NOW CUP CULEVA AND WHEN DO YOU OPEN") plus 50 ms energy runs, and pin the phrase's word times (`PINS` in that episode's `build_timeline.py`).

## subtitles

- Whisper with `--condition_on_previous_text False` can return a long stretch with no punctuation or capitals (`ag-ui`, 0:26–1:35). Captions then page badly and the SRT splits mid-sentence. Add the punctuation to the caption words (a phrase → mark list in the caption text, then capitalise after `.?!`) instead of patching cues; capitalising every cue made it worse.
- Glossary terms that must stay lower-case (`docker build`) get capitalised by `word_fix` ("docker" → "Docker"): mark them literal in `display` with a `~` prefix (stripped at output). Highlight words that recur light up everywhere unless scoped: put the target in brackets inside a phrase (`'an [image] is'`).
- Script-driven captions (`docker-is-dead`; now `tools/captions/build_timeline.py`): difflib-align the script tokens to the CTC-timed spoken words; equal/replace blocks take the spoken times and show the script token (spelling, casing, punctuation and agreed misspeak fixes come free), script-only words are interpolated, spoken-only words are dropped unless whitelisted (real extras like "the real reason"). Replaced ~60 hand-written FIX/PUNCT/CAP rules and also dropped Whisper's invented repeats. Print the replace/drop report and read it.
- A caption token that keeps its own `!` ("Namoshkar!", "sayonara!") plus the stored punctuation field printed "Namoshkar!!" in the SRT (`system-design-of-ai/05`). Strip the token's trailing `!` before appending the punctuation, and grep every SRT for `!!`, `..` and `,,`. Also split a cue after a comma once it passes ~40 characters: an 84-char cap left 45–52-char lines and a line ending on "the".
- CTC-aligned words can be only ~2 frames long, so a "gap > 18 frames → new cue" rule splits mid-phrase ("Both / cost 25¢", "Next / in AI System Design", `04-cost-metering`). Split on a gap only when the previous word ends in punctuation or the cue already has ≥ 24 characters (`make_srt.py`).
- The `≥ 24 characters` gap split still broke "…rediscovering code your / team already wrote." (a 0.6 s pause after "your"), and a sentence shorter than the 20-character flush ("team already wrote.") then ran into the next sentence in one cue (`Graphify`). Never split on a gap right after an article, possessive or preposition (a, an, the, your, to, of, in, with, and), and close a cue at `.?!` once it has > 8 characters.
- Caption display rules (`DISP`) for short tokens must carry context: a rule `U-P → UP` also turned "adds up to 35%" into "adds UP to" (`sdai-12`). Match the phrase ("U-P and Bihar"), never the bare token.

## qa

- Re-transcribing the delivered (sped-up) video with Whisper to check script coverage: it hallucinates ("Bye. Bye. …" over the end card, "free" for "cheap to write", "REST" for "while the frontend waits" on `graphql-vs-rest`). Settle every missing line with a CTC decode of the final audio (`tools/captions/ctc_window.py`) before calling it missing.
- Sped-up edits (`setpts=PTS/k` + `atempo=k`, output `-r 30`): ffmpeg's CFR output duplicated the first 2 frames, so the picture ran a constant ~60 ms behind the voice (`redis-interview`). Audio checks against the voice track don't catch it. Also compare plate frames against the source frames the EDL + speed map them to (`work/scripts/qa_sync.py` in that project): the best match must be offset 0. Fix: drop the extra leading frames (`trim=start_frame=N,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS`) so the frame count equals round(cut frames / k). Better: put an `fps=30` filter right after `setpts` (`setpts=PTS/k,fps=30`); it gives exactly round(cut frames / k) frames at offset 0 with no trim (`bloom-filter`, 1.10x: 5,830 → 5,300). Don't combine it with the trim, or the picture runs early.
- Lip-sync by jawOpen-vs-audio-envelope correlation is useless for a teleprompter talker: corr 0.04–0.27 with lags −8…+5 frames even on the raw source (`scion`). Measure picture sync instead with motion energy: the output face panel's mean abs frame diff vs the source frames the EDL + speed map to, inside 4–11 s uncut segments, lag search ±8 (`scion` `work/scripts/qa_sync2.py`, corr 0.63–0.87).
- Remotion `OffthreadVideo` with `trimBefore` + `playbackRate` 1.23 showed the picture a constant 1–2 output frames LATE vs the voice (no drift) on `scion`. An advance of 2 source frames over-corrected (median lag −1.5 → +1.5 over 8 segments); 1 source frame centres it. One segment's lag is noisy (±1.5 frames): always take the median over ≥ 8 segments with corr > 0.5 before and after a fix.
- `SYNC_SRC_FRAMES` in `Presenter.tsx` is per plate, not a constant to copy: scion's +1 made `docker-explained`'s picture ~0.7 frames early on average (lags −1…+2). Measure both sides before changing it: `qa_sync2.py` (picture) and `qa_audio_lag.py` (voice envelope vs the source segment, time-scaled: +10 ms on every segment there, so the voice was exact and only the picture offset needed fixing). Same lesson: The "advance 1 source frame" fix from `scion` over-corrected on `jwt-explained` (Remotion 4.0.541, `playbackRate` 1.05): the motion-energy check measured the picture +1 source frame EARLY in 5 of 6 HEAD windows (corr 0.75–0.89). Without the advance it measured −1 (picture late) in 5 of 6 windows, so the true offset is ~½ frame either way: both pass 0 ±1. Measure first; don't chase a half-frame with re-renders. Only correlate FULL-mode windows on a reel: in SPLIT the face is a scaled square crop, and a fixed crop compares the graphic card against the face (corr ≈ 0.1, meaningless).
- The 5 s beat sheet (`fps=1/5,…tile`) samples ~2.5 s AFTER the burned-in timestamp (the `fps` filter rounds to the nearest tick), so a tile stamped 0:10 is the frame at 0:12.5. Read the caption in the tile to place it before calling a scene late or empty (`vectorless-rag`).
- Sheets of the delivered file caught three "base layout arrives late" scenes that the per-scene stills (taken near each scene's end) could not: a title card empty for 0.5 s, bars with nothing on screen for 6 s, a stopwatch row missing for 3.6 s. Also render one still ~12 frames after every scene START.
- The restate test catches "wrong vs right" beats that only read through narration: on `system-design-of-ai/06-cancellation` the fresh agent said the fix is to "listen for req close" (the WRONG one), because the 5 s sample landed while only the struck-through grey `req.on("close")` card was on screen and the right card came 5 s later. Grey + strikethrough was too quiet; a `Stamp` "wrong" on the bad card when the word is said fixed it. Mark a wrong option loudly the moment it's called wrong.
- Fixed-height blocks clip what is inside them: a compact `PromptStack` row (84 px) cut the tool chips' underscores in every request card on `system-design-of-ai/09-prompt-caching`, and the stills QA missed it because the cut chips still read as plain text. Zoom into chip and code rows at full size in the vision pass.
- Never copy a tool into a project. Re-take the episode's HEAD/emphasis spans from its `plan.ts` into `config/qa_sync.json`; a stale phrase fails by name.

## composition

- Code in a 9:16 grid card (972 px wide) needs ≥ 28 px JetBrains Mono to read on a phone; 23–26 px looked fine in the editor and was unreadable at half scale. Wrap long lines and drop trailing comments instead of shrinking the font (`graphql-vs-rest` resolvers card).
- A still `N` frames after a trigger word can land in the next scene; pick still frames inside the scene's own window (between its trigger and the next scene's).
- Draw scenes once in a fixed logical card (972x900) and scale it into the card: in long-form, one scene file serves its 16:9 main and 9:16 trailer. A reel only ever scales into the 9:16 card.
- A dark question card with a kicker ("Today's interview question:") must start TYPING after the words the kicker already shows, but the kicker itself lands on the first spoken word. Typing from the first word duplicated the kicker; delaying both left the card empty for 1.4 s (`rate-limiter`: `kickerAt` prop on Question).
- `at()` throws only at render time, so `tsc` passing proves nothing about phrases. Render one still per scene (a bundle-once still script, half scale) right after writing the scenes; it catches missing phrases and layout collisions in one pass.
- "HEAD, dark style" punch on a portrait talking head (`ag-ui`): keep me full frame, dimmed (`brightness(0.38) saturate(0.6)` via the layout blend), and put the punch graphic on the wall above my head (y ≈ 96–500). Hiding me (bloom-filter's DARK) loses the HEAD beat the brief asked for.
- Whisper on the cut voice parks short words ("a" before "spinner") in the preceding pause, up to 1 s early; video-inspect's `caption_desync` catches it. Pin it from the energy envelope (`pins` in `config/build_timeline.json`), and recheck any trailer segment that starts there.
- A wordmark logo inside a small UI element (a sign-in button, a 56 px lane header) shrinks to unreadable; use the company's official mark there (Google's four-colour "G", saved as `google-g.svg`) and keep the wordmark for logo walls (`oidc`). Likewise, A wide wordmark (MCP 1338×195) in a square logo slot shrinks to a hairline. Give wide logos a wide box (`logoW`), never a square.
- An SVG whose `width`/`height` don't match its `viewBox` aspect (`oidc` `openid.svg`: 320×120 around a portrait viewBox) renders at its declared box. Size the tile to the declared aspect; editing the attributes clipped the wordmark to "Op".
- Lane headers that start larger for an intro beat overlap once three share a 972 px card; move them (spring the y) but keep their size (`SequenceFlow`).
- Hyphenated and possessive words are one token in the word data ("platform-issued", "company's", MFA-merged "a one-time"), so `at("or a platform")` or `at("lets the company")` throws at render time. Write the token as spoken (`at("a-one-time")`, `at("lets the company's")`) and run a phrase check over every `at("…")` against `timeline.json` before any still render (`tools/captions/check_phrases.py`, run from `work/remotion/`): one crash costs a whole bundle + render pass.
- Rows scrolling out of a clipped list leave half-chips under the header; fade the list's top edge with a CSS mask (`linear-gradient(to bottom, transparent 0, black 56px)`).
- Spreading `style={{ opacity: undefined }}` into a component whose own `style` sets opacity from its spring wipes the entrance (React drops the key). Wrap the component in a div for extra opacity or filter instead.
- An unscoped `at("its client id")` resolves to the FIRST time I say it: in `oidc` Part 2 that was the login step 2 line, 1 min early, so a later scene showed a row before I spoke it. Scope repeated phrases to their beat (`at(phrase, from)`); the phrase check lists every unscoped phrase spoken more than once.
- Remotion composition ids allow only a-z, A-Z, 0-9 and `-`: `Short_p1` throws at bundle time (validateCompositionId), and every still/render in that batch dies with it. Use `Short-p1`.
- Word data stores number words as digits ("one" → `1`, "five" → `5`, "fifty" → `50`), so `at("best one first")` and `at("two indexes")` throw. Apply the same number map inside `at()` and the phrase check, and in the highlight-phrase matcher, or highlight words like "top five" silently never light up (`rag-pipeline`). Also: The phrase lookup's number map turns "Three" into "3", so `at('Three')` matched "under 3 minutes" in the hook and idea 3's card rendered over every scene from 0:09 on (`scion`). Anchor numbered list items with the next word (`at('Three source')`) or a `from` frame.
- Running canvas (`components/specs/PipelineCanvas.md`): stations have transparent backgrounds, so connectors drawn centre-to-centre cut through titles and gauges. Draw them edge-to-edge inside the gaps.

## video-inspect

- Never name the script `inspect.py`: it shadows the stdlib `inspect`, and numpy's import then fails as a circular import of scenedetect.
- Phone footage stores 1920x1080 with a 90° rotation flag, and ffmpeg auto-rotates on decode. Read `stream_side_data=rotation` (or the `rotate` tag) and swap W/H, or the summary says landscape while every frame is portrait (IntroVideo `main.mp4`).
- moondream (100% GPU, 1.2 GB) captioned me as "urn of flowers on a desk…" on IntroVideo; qwen2.5vl:3b got "A man in a blue hoodie… talking". Use qwen. On the 4 GB GTX 1650 it runs ~57% on the GPU whatever `num_ctx` is (the weights don't fit): ~25 s to load, then fast per frame. That's a hardware limit, stated in the report's notes, not a silent fallback. Use `qwen2.5vl:7b` on ≥ 8 GB.
- An Ollama model still loaded from earlier (keep-alive) holds ~2 GB: Whisper then dies with "CUDA failed with error out of memory". The script unloads Ollama first.
- `ollama run <model> "prompt"` from a non-TTY shell with open stdin waits forever. Use the HTTP API, or `< /dev/null`.
- `pip check` reports "scenedetect requires opencv-python". That's expected: it was installed `--no-deps` and uses the headless OpenCV.
- The first PaddleOCR run downloads PP-OCRv5 mobile into `~/.paddlex/official_models` (~30 s). After that it does ~0.1 s per frame in ~0.4 GB VRAM.
- B-roll / vlog footage (no talking head) also needs a quality pass, which the table above doesn't cover: at 5 fps on 480 px greyscale, Laplacian variance (sharpness), mean luma (dark < 40, blown > 215), `cv2.phaseCorrelate` shift between frames minus its 1 s moving average (camera shake, % of width), and mean abs frame diff (content motion). Report it per scene and per second; it's what ranks shots. No tracked implementation exists yet.
- On an edited cut, every hard cut reads as a huge phase-correlation jump, so `shaky` fired on 9 cuts of `fannout-day-01`'s rough cut. Zero the jitter within 0.4 s of each scene boundary.
- `freezedetect` prints no `freeze_end` when the freeze runs to the end of the file (an end card, a slate). Close it at the video's duration, or the report shows a nonsense length.
- Phone desk footage can show private screens (Gmail inbox, Google account recovery on `fannout-day-01`). Look at the monitors on the contact sheet before you plan any punch-in on a screen, and blur them when you're unsure.
- No flag catches a scene that was never built. On `redis-invalidation` Part 1, the storyboard beat D8 (Facebook memcache leases) had no component, so the SPLIT grid card sat empty for ~6 s and inspect raised nothing. Before the final render, check every storyboard row against a component, and on the contact sheet look for blank cards in SPLIT.
- `text_over_face` with tiny OCR strings like `ATX3`, `AYAD`, `AT23` is the red wall sticker behind me (and the signature at the end), not overlay text. Treat it as a false positive.
- `caption_desync` pairs a caption's first word with the nearest same word within 2 s, so a short word ("a", "the") can match an earlier occurrence and report +1.7 s (`jwt-explained` "a JWKS"). Check the timeline word time before acting. `misspelling` fires on intentional strings (a JWT token, `wordlist.txt`): treat code/token text as false positives.
- PySceneDetect finds very few scenes on a reel whose cuts are spring morphs (9 scenes in 2:51), so keyframes alone miss most beats: always look at the 48-tile contact sheet as well.
- Once captions are CTC-aligned (`pipeline/transcribe.md`), `caption_desync` at +0.5…+0.7 s is Whisper's early-start bias, not a late caption: on `jwt-explained` (thorough, 6 fps band) all 19 flags had the caption within 1–3 frames of the CTC onset. Check flagged words against the CTC word times before changing anything; consider comparing to CTC-corrected starts in the script itself. Same on `sdai-03` (51 flags, +0.6…+1.4 s): frame grabs at the timeline word start showed the karaoke word already lit; the +1 s+ ones were footer chips under the card ("LLM call ×4", "Same meaning → close together") read as captions. A fast check: grab the caption band at the timeline start of 2–3 flagged words and look. Again: `caption_desync` reported a median +0.7 s on `system-design-of-ai/08-long-running-requests` (44 flags) while the captions actually led speech by 0.03 s at every clean energy onset (20 ms envelope of `audio/voice.wav`). Check a handful of flagged starts against the envelope before touching the timeline; the flag also fires on FULL-card footers (FootCap text), which aren't captions.
- Back-to-back inspect runs in one shell loop can OOM at the next run's Whisper load while the previous process is still freeing VRAM. Wait for an empty `--query-compute-apps` between runs.
- `--thorough` grabs 1 s keyframes up to the end; a frame at t = duration (153.00 s of a 153.033 s file) comes back empty and crashed face detection (`cv2.cvtColor … !_src.empty()`) on `system-design-of-ai/10-batch-inference`. The keyframe loop stops at `DUR − 0.1` (kept in `tools/inspect/video_inspect.py`).

## render

- Stop a render by PID (`pgrep -f 'remotion render'`, then `kill <pid>`), never `pkill -f <script name>` from the same shell: the pattern also matches your own command line and kills it (`sdai-12`).
- Check the voice WAV's start offset against the timeline. A mis-laid voice track once started 2.5–4 s late, and only the A/V check in `qa.md` caught it.
- Keep masters until I approve the finals, so re-encoding never means re-rendering.
- A looped still input (`-loop 1 -i mask.png`) never ends, so `-shortest` alone doesn't stop a `-filter_complex` encode: one ran for 1 h and wrote 1.4 GB. Add `shortest=1` to every framesync filter (`overlay`, `alphamerge`) and `-frames:v <source frames>` as a hard cap.
- Never edit a stage script while a runner is executing it: bash reads the file as it goes, so the running job picked up half an edit ("JOBS: unbound variable") and skipped every encode (`kafka`). Edit a copy, or wait for the job to finish.
- Project folders may be renamed mid-project with shell-special characters (`oidc` → `oidc-6&7.11.26`). Stage scripts must not hardcode the project path: derive it (`W="$(cd "$(dirname "$0")/.." && pwd)"`) and quote every path, or an unquoted `&` backgrounds half the command. Tell the Haiku runner to `cd` with the quoted path.
- A 1080x1920 portrait source with ~44 `OffthreadVideo` segments grew the Remotion compositor to 8.8 GB and the kernel OOM-killed it at frame 1,350 (`jwt-explained`, while another session's render held 5.6 GB). Pass `--offthreadvideo-cache-size-in-bytes=2000000000` and `--concurrency=3`, and check `free -h` for other projects' renders before starting. Stopping a chain: kill by PID; `pkill -f <project path>` also matches (and kills) your own shell.
- A background render chain can outlive the shell that launched it: on `docker-explained` the notifying shell died (exit 144) while `render_all.sh` kept going, and a manual re-run of its delivery step wrote the same MP4s at the same time. Both "finished" with exit 0 and a valid-looking ebur128, but the files had corrupt H.264 NAL units and an undecodable AAC track (−70 LUFS on re-read). Before re-running any step by hand, `pgrep -f <chain script>`; run chains only through `tools/jobs/batch.sh` (one batch per project) and wait for it to end; verify every delivery with `ffmpeg -v error -i out.mp4 -f null -` (no output = clean).
- Remotion's default 28 s `delayRender` timeout can expire while `OffthreadVideo` seeks a 1.2 GB 1080x1920 NVENC working copy, killing the whole render at frame 0 (`jwt-is-dead`: both trailers, which nest Main several times, and one still batch). Pass `--timeout=120000` to `remotion render` and `timeoutInMilliseconds: 120000` to `renderStill`.

## gpu

- On a 4 GB card (GTX 1650), Whisper large-v3 does not fit in float16. Use `compute_type int8_float16`.
- MediaPipe's Python face detector runs on the CPU only. That is expected; report it rather than hiding it.
- Run one heavy GPU job at a time on small cards. While one runs, small side jobs (stills, thumbnails, rembg, contact sheets) go on the CPU instead of waiting: Remotion without `gpu-run` and with `--gl=swangle`. Log that they ran on the CPU (CLAUDE.md exception).
- If a check fails, find the cause (driver, PATH, missing libs). If it truly cannot be fixed, stop and tell me.
- The card may be shared with another Claude session (another project transcribing at the same time). An OOM in Whisper usually means someone else holds VRAM: check `nvidia-smi --query-compute-apps=pid,used_memory` and `readlink /proc/<pid>/cwd`. Never kill another project's job; wait for it, or transcribe short windows (a few seconds each fit beside it). Two sessions sharing the card take turns badly: another project's back-to-back Whisper passes OOM'd our final-voice Whisper and CTC three times (`docker-explained`). Wrap every GPU step in a wait-and-retry helper: block until `nvidia-smi --query-compute-apps=process_name` lists no `python`, run, and retry on "out of memory" (`tools/jobs/gpu_wait.sh`). A second session's Remotion render can also start beside yours; it only slows both, so leave it.
- Whisper large-v3 at `int8` peaked at ~3.3 GB on the GTX 1650 on one video, but see the next point: it is not always the safer choice.
- `whisper-ctranslate2` exits 0 on "CUDA failed with error out of memory". Judge a pass by its output JSON existing, not the exit code. Wait for an empty `--query-compute-apps` before each pass (another project may run passes back to back) and retry a pass that left no JSON.
- Whisper OOM at the same spot on every try, alone on a free card, is the temperature fallback: it samples `best_of` (default 5) candidates at once. On `graphql-vs-rest` both `int8` and `int8_float16` died ~1 min in; `--best_of 1` finished at 2.2 GB. Pass `--best_of 1` by default on a 4 GB card.
- A wait loop like `while pgrep -f whisper-ctranslate2` matches its own shell's command line and never ends. Match the interpreter path instead (`pgrep -f '^/home/…/venv/bin/python3'`).
- `int8_float16` can OOM even on a 40 s clip at ~2.6 GB with a free card (`jwt-explained`, GTX 1650, large-v3, beam 5): the error lands right after "Detected language". `--compute_type int8` (beam 5) and `int8_float16 --beam_size 2` both finished. Test a 40 s window first when a pass leaves no JSON.

## trailer

- Simplest build: replay the main composition per segment, `<Sequence from={offset} durationInFrames={b - a}><Sequence from={-a}><Main trailer /></Sequence></Sequence>`. Voice, SFX, captions and scenes stay in sync; hide anything that would jump between segments (a running countdown) with a `trailer` prop (`graphql-vs-rest`).
- End a replayed segment 2 frames after its last word when the main's next line starts right after it: the default 8-frame tail caught the next scene's fade and the first caption word (`rag-pipeline`). Start a segment exactly on its first word for the same reason.
- Replaying Main per segment opens each segment on the last frames of the PREVIOUS main scene's fade-out when that scene isn't in the trailer (`vectorless-rag`: the R1 segment began with a ghost of T2's "WON'T FIT" for ~6 frames). Give scenes a trailer flag (a React context set by `Main`'s `trailer` prop) that cuts them 3 frames before their `out` instead of cross-fading, and end a segment at `min(last word + 4, next scene start − 10)` so it never runs into that cut (an empty card showed for the last frames of K2).
- A dark trailer `EndCard` that fades in from black reads as a black flash after a bright HEAD beat (video-inspect `black_flash` 0.13 s). Cut it in; fade only its exit.
