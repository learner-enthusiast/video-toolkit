# LowerThird

**Purpose:** name and title the first time I appear.

**Props:** `name`, `title`, `at`, `out`, `side`.

**Look**
- A brand card anchored at the bottom-left safe margin, on the free side of the presenter, never over the face.
- Name in Inter 700 at about 48 px, title in Inter 500 dim at about 28 px, and a 4 px accent bar on the left edge.

**Motion**
- The accent bar grows first (spring), then the name slides in from 24 px left with a fade, then the title follows 5 frames later.
- Exit: reverse fade and slide, 10 frames.
- Hold for 3–4 s, triggered on my name being spoken.

**Gotchas**
- Push the captions up while it is visible, or time it so they don't collide.
- In 9:16, put it above the caption band, not at the very bottom (platform UI covers that area).
