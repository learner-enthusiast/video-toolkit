# Checklist

**Purpose:** a recap list that builds line by line with orange ticks, one line per spoken phrase.

**Props:** `items` (bold term, dim note, `at`), `rect`, `out`.

**Look**
- Lines in Inter: term in 700 near-black, note in 400 warm grey after a mid-dot. A round orange tick at the left of each.

**Motion**
- Each line slides in from the left 24 px with a spring at its phrase; the tick draws its stroke (~8 frames).
- Exit: lines fade with a slight lift, staggered 4 frames.
