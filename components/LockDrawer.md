# LockDrawer

**Purpose:** analogy: JWT gives a drawer of locks ("pick one"), PASETO one fitted lock per version.

**Props:** `at`, `out`, `jwtAt`, `pickAt`, `pasetoAt`, `fittedAt`, `rect`.

**Look**
- Left half "JWT": an open drawer (line drawing) overflowing with assorted lock icons of different shapes, warm grey; pill "pick one" with a question mark.
- Right half "PASETO": four doors (v1–v4 labels) each with one solid orange lock already fitted; tag "already fitted".
- A thin divider; headers in Inter 900.

**Motion:** locks tumble into the drawer with staggered springs (4 frames); PASETO locks snap onto doors one by one. Exit fade.
