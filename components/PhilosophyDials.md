# PhilosophyDials

**Purpose:** PASETO's origin and design rule: no choices, nothing to downgrade to.

**Props:** `at`, `out`, `nameAt` (wordmark + expansion), `creditAt` (author card), `quoteAt`, `dialsAt`, `offAt` ([negotiation, configurable crypto] switch-off frames), `ladderAt`, `rungsAt` (lower rungs drop), `rect`.

**Look**
- "PASETO" wordmark: Inter 900, near-black, letter-spaced, with the expansion "Platform-Agnostic SEcurity TOkens" below, the capitals P/A/SE/TO in orange. No logo exists, so no logo is drawn.
- Credit card: white panel "Scott Arciszewski · Paragon Initiative Enterprises · March 2018" (Inter 600, 34 px).
- Quote card: Instrument Serif Italic "“enough rope to hang themselves”", attribution line "— Scott Arciszewski, on JOSE/JWT" (dim).
- Two toggle switches (pill track, 2 px border): label "negotiation", "configurable crypto"; ON = orange knob right, OFF = grey track, knob left, "OFF" label.
- Ladder line drawing labelled "downgrade": its lower rungs fall away; caption pill "nothing to downgrade to" (orange).

**Motion:** sections stack in spoken order; earlier ones compress upward (spring) to make room; switches flip with a spring (knob slides 10 frames). Rungs drop with gravity-ish ease-in then fade. Exit fade.
